# Third-party adapter candidates

The default research config includes candidates from third-party Hugging Face repositories.

Fleet does not claim ownership of those weights.

A compiled derivative should only be redistributed after confirming that the source license
permits the intended redistribution and that attribution requirements are satisfied.

The runtime records the Hub-reported license in `fleet_bank.json`.

Unknown-license adapters may be used for local experiments when configured, but should not
be promoted to a public artifact automatically.
